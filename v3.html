What I do

I find the angle, write the ad, then multiply what wins.

01

Find the angle

Before a word is written I read the customer in their own words: reviews, comments, forums. The ad speaks in their sentences, not the brand’s.

02

Write the ad

The concept, three hooks, the full script and the edit note. One brief an editor can cut without a call.

03

Multiply what wins

When an ad wins I take it apart and rebuild it for the next person and the next problem. One winning story, moved to a new product, sold another $118,970.

Seven of my eight biggest ads are story songs: a sung story, a few minutes long, animated with AI.

Who I am

I’m Ben Lewis. I write and direct ads that sell.
